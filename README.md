# SmartParcours
## Complete Product Vision and Functional Presentation
### An intelligent student orientation, academic identity, and educational opportunity platform

## 1. Executive Summary

SmartParcours is a mobile-first, bilingual educational platform designed to help students understand their academic profiles, discover their potential, make informed educational decisions, and connect with people and institutions that can support their future.

The platform combines artificial intelligence, academic records, personalized orientation, university exploration, professional guidance, and a structured educational community in one accessible environment.

SmartParcours is built around the student rather than the school. Every student can create an account independently, maintain a personal academic profile, record their grades, receive AI-assisted orientation recommendations, discover universities, participate in communities, and access educational opportunities.

Schools, teachers, professionals, and universities can join the platform to provide additional value. Schools can verify academic information, teachers and professionals can provide guidance and comments, and universities can communicate with interested students and offer admission-related opportunities.

Institutional participation does not determine whether a student can use the core platform. A student whose school has no SmartParcours account can still create a profile, receive orientation recommendations, participate in the community, and explore universities.

The platform supports French and English, with an interface designed primarily for mobile devices while remaining fully responsive on desktop computers.

**SmartParcours helps students understand their potential, explore their possibilities, and connect with the people and opportunities that can help them progress.**

## 2. The Problem SmartParcours Addresses

Students often need to make important educational decisions without enough personalized guidance, reliable information, or access to people who can help.

SmartParcours addresses several related challenges.

### 2.1. Educational orientation is not always personalized

Grades alone do not fully describe a student's interests, motivations, aspirations, preferences, or potential areas of development.

A student may perform well in several subjects without knowing which educational pathway to explore. Another student may have interests that connect to scientific or technical fields they have never considered.

SmartParcours combines academic information, questionnaires, AI analysis, and human guidance to help students explore more informed possibilities.

### 2.2. Educational opportunities can be difficult to discover

Information about universities, programmes, scholarships, admission requirements, and career pathways is often scattered across different institutions and websites.

SmartParcours brings educational information and personalized recommendations into a more accessible environment.

### 2.3. Academic records should follow the student

Students may need to collect and submit academic documents repeatedly when changing schools or applying to new institutions.

SmartParcours provides a persistent student profile that can retain academic history across school changes. When formal verification is required, the platform facilitates a verification process with the relevant school.

### 2.4. Students need access to guidance and knowledge

Students may need help from teachers, orientation specialists, professionals, or peers who have relevant experience.

SmartParcours provides moderated communities and professional knowledge spaces where users can ask questions, exchange resources, and receive guidance without relying on private direct messaging.

### 2.5. Educational platforms should not depend entirely on school adoption

Students should not be excluded simply because their school has not adopted a digital platform.

SmartParcours allows independent registration and personal use. Schools can participate when they are ready to provide institutional verification and other authorized services.

## 3. Product Philosophy and Core Values

SmartParcours is built around eight principles.

**Student ownership.** Students manage their own accounts and maintain a portable academic history.

**Accessible by default.** School participation is not required to use the essential platform features.

**Intelligent but human-supported orientation.** AI provides personalized recommendations, while relevant human feedback can help contextualize, validate, and improve guidance.

**Freedom to explore.** Recommendations help students consider possibilities rather than dictate a single future.

**Trusted academic information.** Student-entered information, uploaded documents, and officially verified records are clearly distinguished.

**Community and shared knowledge.** Students, educators, professionals, and universities can contribute knowledge through structured public spaces.

**Privacy and protection.** Direct messaging between users is not supported. Personal information, academic records, comments, and profile sharing have appropriate controls.

**Fair access.** A student's gender, school participation, game performance, or financial circumstances should not determine their academic worth or restrict their essential access to orientation.

## 4. Main Navigation and Platform Pages

The student-facing platform contains seven principal sections and a dedicated support area.

### 4.1. My Profile

The student's academic and personal identity, including school history, grades, interests, achievements, orientation summaries, comments, and profile-sharing controls.

### 4.2. My Orientation

The central intelligent system that combines relevant academic information, questionnaire responses, and appropriate human feedback to generate personalized educational and career recommendations.

### 4.3. Community

A collection of student communities, educator and professional Knowledge Hubs, university forums, shared resources, and a personalized activity feed.

### 4.4. Explore Universities

A directory of universities, programmes, admission requirements, scholarships, and institutional opportunities, with access to participating universities' admission-related processes.

### 4.5. Documentation

The official user guide for SmartParcours. It explains how the platform works, how to register and log in, how to use each feature, and what each account role can do.

Educational resources such as tutorials, study tips, and learning documents belong in Community, where users can publish, discover, filter, and save them.

### 4.6. Subscribe

The area for viewing optional paid services, purchasing additional AI-generation credits, and managing eligible subscriptions or credit balances.

The core student experience is free, with a defined limit on AI recommendation generations.

### 4.7. Support — Ask SmartParcours

A dedicated support area where users can ask questions about the platform, report technical problems, request help with accounts or verification, and follow support requests where available.

### 4.8. Language selection

The interface supports **French and English**. Users should be able to select their preferred language and change it later.

The platform should aim for consistent terminology, navigation, support documentation, and user guidance in both languages. Educational content and institutional information may be available in one language only when no verified translation exists.

## 5. My Profile: The Student's Persistent Academic Identity

My Profile is one of the foundations of SmartParcours.

Each student creates and manages a personal account independently of their school. Their profile remains theirs when they change institutions or progress to another class.

### 5.1. Profile information

The profile can contain:

- Name and necessary account information.
- Current school and class.
- Previous school history where relevant.
- Academic year and class progression.
- Bulletin records and general averages.
- Interests, aspirations, and selected personal information.
- Orientation summaries and recommendations selected for display.
- Discovery achievements and points, where enabled.
- Teacher and professional comments.
- Public-profile visibility settings.
- Links to verified academic records where available.

Sensitive information is private by default. Students should be able to understand what is public, what is private, and what is included in a shared link.

### 5.2. School information

During registration, students select their school from a recognized school directory.

If their school is not listed, they can submit a request to add it. SmartParcours reviews new entries to prevent duplicate records and misleading institutional identities.

Each recognized school has a canonical record. When an authorized school representative updates its official name or other shared information, the change is reflected consistently across associated student profiles and relevant platform features.

Changing one student's school affiliation does not change the affiliation of other students.

### 5.3. Academic bulletins

Students can enter grades manually or upload a photograph or PDF of a school bulletin.

Where supported, AI-assisted document processing extracts relevant information such as:

- Subject names and grades.
- Coefficients and general averages.
- Class and academic year.
- Relevant comments appearing on the document.

Students can review and correct extracted information before saving it.

The platform distinguishes between student-entered information, information extracted from an uploaded document, and information verified by an authorized school representative.

An uploaded bulletin is not automatically an officially verified record.

### 5.4. Portable academic history

The student's account remains available when they change schools.

They can update their current school and class while retaining previous academic records. Where a school has participated in verification, the relevant verified record and its history can remain associated with the student's profile.

### 5.5. Three profile-sharing experiences

SmartParcours provides different profile views for different purposes.

#### A. Verified academic profile

This version is intended for formal processes such as school transfers, university applications, scholarships, or other situations requiring verified academic information.

It may contain:

- Relevant student identification information.
- Current and previous school information.
- Academic history and detailed bulletins.
- General averages and subject-level results.
- Verification status and issuing school.
- Verification date and record version.

The student requests verification through SmartParcours. The relevant school administrator reviews the information and confirms the accuracy of the academic records within their authority.

After approval, SmartParcours issues a dated, versioned profile or academic record with a shareable link.

A previously verified record must not silently change when a student edits their profile. Any new verification should create a distinguishable version or record.

A receiving university or institution must explicitly agree to accept a SmartParcours verified profile for its formal process. Verification does not automatically make it an accepted official document everywhere.

#### B. Public student profile

This version allows students to present themselves, their achievements, their interests, and selected academic information.

It can contain:

- A student introduction.
- Selected academic results or general averages.
- Interests and aspirations.
- Selected orientation recommendations.
- Completed activities and achievements.
- Gamification points.
- Teacher and professional comments.

The public profile does not automatically expose detailed bulletins, private questionnaire responses, or full orientation reports.

Students can choose which sections appear publicly and can hide comments or other permitted sections.

#### C. Teacher and professional feedback

Teachers and verified professionals can contribute comments through the platform's designated feedback process.

These comments may describe a student's academic strengths, work, progress, achievements, or areas where further development could be useful.

**Teacher and professional comments are visible on the public profile by default**, unless the student chooses to hide them or reports them.

Reporting a comment sends it for review under SmartParcours' moderation rules. A report should not automatically be treated as proof of misconduct; the platform should assess the case and take appropriate action.

The platform distinguishes ordinary public feedback from formal recommendations and records the contributor's role and verification status where applicable.

### 5.6. Profile privacy

Students control the information displayed in public profile views and the information shared for formal purposes.

Where appropriate, shared links can be revoked or replaced. The platform should explain that revoking a link cannot necessarily retrieve information that a recipient has already downloaded or copied.

For minors, appropriate safeguards apply to profile visibility, institutional access, and interactions with adults.

## 6. My Orientation: The Core Intelligent System

My Orientation is the central intelligence of SmartParcours.

Its purpose is to help students understand their academic profiles, discover educational pathways, and make better-informed decisions about their future.

The system combines academic records, personal interests, aspirations, questionnaire responses, and relevant human feedback to produce personalized recommendations.

### 6.1. Information used for orientation

Depending on what the student has provided and consented to use, the system may consider:

- Academic results across subjects and school years.
- Subject-level strengths and areas for development.
- General averages and progression.
- Interests and preferred activities.
- Educational and career aspirations.
- Learning preferences.
- Preferred study duration and learning environment.
- Work and career motivations.
- Relevant questionnaire responses.
- Human comments from teachers and professionals.
- Educational constraints or preferences where relevant.

The platform should explain which information contributes to a recommendation and allow students to correct inaccurate or outdated information.

### 6.2. Questionnaires

SmartParcours builds on its existing questionnaire-based approach, including an initial questionnaire covering academic, aspiration, and logic-related categories, alongside a more detailed questionnaire exploring academic preferences, personal motivations, and reasoning-related questions.

Questionnaires should be understandable for the student's age and educational level. Progress should be saved so students can pause and resume later.

The system should not present ordinary questionnaires as definitive psychological or intelligence tests unless they have been appropriately validated for that purpose.

### 6.3. AI-generated recommendations

The AI system analyzes the available information and produces a structured orientation report that may include:

- A summary of the student's academic profile.
- Identified strengths and areas for development.
- Subjects and fields worth exploring.
- Suggested study pathways.
- Potential careers and related skills.
- Explanations of why each pathway may be relevant.
- Possible educational institutions or programmes.
- Practical next steps.

The system should provide alternatives rather than forcing a student into a single category. Recommendations should acknowledge uncertainty when information is incomplete.

### 6.4. Human-supported AI orientation

AI is useful for analyzing information and generating individualized guidance, but its recommendations can be incomplete or lack context.

SmartParcours therefore allows relevant human feedback from teachers, orientation specialists, and professionals to contribute to the orientation process.

Human contributions can help the system:

- Understand a student's context more accurately.
- Add observations that grades and questionnaires may not capture.
- Identify relevant skills, interests, or experiences.
- Suggest alternative pathways.
- Review recommendations that appear incomplete or unsuitable.
- Provide explanations grounded in educational or professional experience.

The platform may use authorized human comments as input to a subsequent recommendation generation or as context for a human-reviewed recommendation.

However, **human feedback should complement AI, not automatically be treated as unquestionable truth**. The platform must distinguish a verified contributor's identity from the correctness of their opinion, and it should not treat every public comment as authoritative evidence.

Only comments relevant to orientation and appropriately authorized for that purpose should be used as inputs. Students should understand how their comments and records may be used.

The system should preserve the distinction between the AI's analysis, the human contributor's opinion, and officially verified academic facts.

### 6.5. Educational information matching

The system can connect suggested pathways to available programmes, universities, admission requirements, and educational resources.

This information must be maintained responsibly. AI-generated suggestions should not invent admission requirements, deadlines, or institutional facts.

### 6.6. Updating recommendations

Students can update their grades, interests, questionnaire responses, and other relevant information over time.

When appropriate, they can generate a new recommendation that takes these changes into account.

The platform should avoid encouraging repeated generations that provide little additional value and should explain the basis of updated recommendations.

### 6.7. AI transparency and fairness

AI recommendations are guidance, not official judgments about a student's potential.

The system must not automatically exclude students from a field based on gender, school background, grades, or a predicted compatibility score.

If compatibility percentages are used, they should be explained as estimates produced by the recommendation system rather than objective probabilities of success.

SmartParcours should evaluate recommendation quality across different student groups and educational contexts. Students should be able to question, correct, and seek human input on their recommendations.

### 6.8. AI beyond orientation

Other potential uses include:

- Extracting and organizing information from uploaded bulletins.
- Helping students understand documentation.
- Finding relevant resources.
- Assisting with university and programme comparisons.
- Supporting moderation workflows.
- Detecting possible duplicate school records for human review.
- Helping classify support requests.

These functions are secondary to the core orientation system.

AI must not independently certify official academic records, approve school identities, make university admission decisions, or replace human judgment in consequential processes.

## 7. Community: The Social and Knowledge-Sharing Core

Community is one of the central pillars of SmartParcours.

It allows students to ask questions, exchange knowledge, discover resources, and connect with peers, educators, professionals, and universities.

### 7.1. Community structure

SmartParcours can support several types of community:

- **School communities:** spaces for students associated with a particular school.
- **Class-level communities:** discussions among students in the same class level across different schools.
- **National student communities:** broader discussions among students across Madagascar.
- **General communities:** spaces open to eligible platform users.
- **Knowledge Hubs:** professional and educational spaces for teachers, orientation specialists, and professionals.
- **University forums:** official spaces for university announcements, programmes, events, and questions.

Users can browse and join relevant communities according to the rules of each space.

### 7.2. Knowledge Hubs

Teachers and professionals can contribute to structured spaces for:

- Study advice and learning strategies.
- Educational orientation.
- Career exploration.
- Practical skills and professional experience.
- Mentorship and guidance.
- Subject-specific questions.
- Educational opportunities.

Contributors can publish resources, answer questions, and share relevant experience.

Their identity and credentials should be verified to the degree appropriate for the role they claim.

### 7.3. Educational resources inside Community

Resources are part of Community rather than a separate general resource library.

Users can publish or share:

- Tutorials.
- Study notes and explanations.
- Learning documents.
- Educational videos or links.
- Practical guides.
- Career and university information.
- Other useful educational material.

Community should support filtering resources by subject, topic, education level, or other relevant categories. Students can save resources for later access.

The platform should distinguish official information from community contributions and provide tools to report inaccurate, unsafe, or inappropriate material.

### 7.4. Combined activity feed

A personalized feed can combine relevant updates from communities and university forums the student follows.

It may display:

- New discussions.
- Answers to questions.
- Shared educational resources.
- Professional advice.
- University announcements.
- Scholarships and other opportunities.
- Discovery-game updates.
- Important platform announcements.

Students should be able to manage followed spaces and notification preferences.

### 7.5. No direct messaging between users

**SmartParcours does not support direct private messaging between users.**

This is a deliberate safety decision because many users will be young students.

Communication should take place through moderated public communities, permitted comments, structured feedback requests, and other controlled workflows.

The platform should not provide an unrestricted private chat channel between students, teachers, professionals, university representatives, or other users.

Students can ask questions publicly without revealing their entire profile. They can share selected profile information where appropriate, but the platform should not require public disclosure to obtain help.

### 7.6. Comments and profile feedback

Profile comments are reserved for permitted teachers and professionals.

These comments appear publicly by default unless the student hides them or reports them. Reported comments are handled under moderation procedures.

Student-to-student discussions take place in Community rather than as unrestricted comments on individual profiles.

This separation keeps student profiles more professional while allowing peer discussion elsewhere.

### 7.7. Moderation and safety

Community requires reporting tools, moderation rules, protection against harassment, and safeguards for minors.

The platform should provide clear rules governing public content, professional conduct, school-related discussions, and interactions with young users.

Any closer guidance relationship must operate within a defined, safe workflow rather than relying on unrestricted private messaging.

## 8. Explore Universities: Educational Opportunities and Admission Processes

Explore Universities helps students discover institutions, programmes, admission requirements, and scholarship opportunities.

### 8.1. University directory

Each university profile may include:

- Official name and location.
- Programmes and courses.
- Entry requirements.
- Application procedures and deadlines.
- Fees and funding information where available.
- Scholarships.
- Official contact information and application links.
- Institutional announcements and events.

Information should be sourced and maintained responsibly. Unverified or outdated requirements should not be presented as current facts.

### 8.2. University accounts

Universities can create institutional accounts after verification by SmartParcours.

Authorized representatives can maintain the university profile, publish announcements, manage official university forums, and participate in permitted admission-related workflows.

### 8.3. University forums

Each participating university can maintain a dedicated community or forum where interested students can follow updates, ask questions publicly, and discover programmes and events.

Universities can share official announcements and educational opportunities without needing to send private messages to individual students.

### 8.4. Expressing interest

Students can express interest in a university or programme through SmartParcours.

When a student expresses interest without submitting a formal pre-admission request, the student can be included in the university's list of interested students, subject to the student's consent and the applicable information-sharing rules.

This allows universities to understand student interest and communicate relevant information through their official platform spaces.

An expression of interest is not an application, pre-admission decision, or admission offer.

### 8.5. University interest in students

Participating universities may discover eligible students or express interest in their profiles, subject to privacy controls.

Students can see the number of universities that have expressed interest in their profile where this feature is enabled.

SmartParcours must distinguish between profile interest, an invitation to apply, preliminary selection, pre-admission, and formal admission.

Universities do not receive unrestricted access to private student records merely because they have an account.

### 8.6. Formal pre-admission

A student can request a formal pre-admission process through SmartParcours when a participating university offers that service.

The process requires the student's verified academic profile, because the institution needs an appropriate basis for reviewing the student's academic history.

The university determines its eligibility requirements, reviews the application, and decides the outcome. SmartParcours facilitates the process and displays relevant statuses but does not independently confer admission.

The platform should clearly identify the required documents, the applicable fee, and the status of the request.

### 8.7. Scholarships and scholarship sponsors

SmartParcours aims to work more closely with scholarship providers and organizations supporting educational access.

Participating sponsors may help identify outstanding students based on transparent, agreed criteria and the information students have consented to share.

The platform can help students discover relevant scholarship opportunities and facilitate appropriate connections between students and sponsors.

Academic performance, personal circumstances, interests, or other selection criteria must be used according to each sponsor's stated requirements. AI-generated recommendations should not be represented as a scholarship award or an official selection decision.

Scholarship sponsors must not receive unrestricted access to student records. The student should understand what information is being shared and why.

Documentation is dedicated to explaining SmartParcours itself.

It is not the main repository for educational resources, tutorials about school subjects, or community-contributed learning material. Those belong in Community.

The Documentation section should help users understand the platform thoroughly.

It may include:

- How to register and log in.
- How to recover account access.
- How to complete and update a student profile.
- How to enter grades and upload bulletins.
- How to use My Orientation.
- How AI recommendations work and what their limitations are.
- How to share a public profile.
- How to request a verified academic profile.
- How schools process verification requests.
- How teachers and professionals register and contribute.
- How universities create and manage their accounts.
- How to participate in Community.
- How to publish and save educational resources.
- How leaderboards and points work.
- How Subscribe and additional credits work.
- How to use Support.
- Privacy, safety, reporting, and account-management guidance.

Documentation should explain each account role and the permissions associated with it.

The guide should be available in French and English and should be kept consistent with the actual platform experience.

## 10. Subscribe: A Free Student Platform with Optional Paid Services

SmartParcours is free for students to use, with a defined limit on AI recommendation generations.

The business model focuses on optional additional generations, university participation, and formal pre-admission services rather than charging students for basic access.

### 10.1. Free student access

Students can access the platform without a subscription fee.

The free experience includes the core profile, academic information management, orientation features within the generation limit, university browsing, Community, Documentation, and Support.

The future Discover experience will also be designed around accessible participation rather than requiring payment for the core educational journey.

### 10.2. AI recommendation generation limit

Each student receives **three AI recommendation generations per trimester**.

Students can purchase additional credits to generate more recommendations when needed.

The platform should explain when a generation is consumed and show the student's remaining allowance.

A new generation should provide a meaningful opportunity to incorporate updated information, revised questionnaire answers, or relevant human feedback.

The platform should avoid charging students for accidental duplicate requests or failed generations that did not produce a usable result.

### 10.3. Additional credits

Students can purchase additional credits through the Subscribe section.

Credits are used for eligible additional AI recommendation generations. Other paid uses should only be introduced when they are clearly defined.

Earned game points and purchased credits must remain separate. Discovery Points reflect participation in the game; purchased credits are a payment-based service entitlement.

A student's payment status must not change the academic credibility of their profile or influence formal verification and admission decisions.

### 10.4. University participation fee

Universities pay a relatively small annual fee to maintain a participating institutional presence on SmartParcours.

The university account may provide access to the university profile, official forum, programme information, and other agreed institutional features.

The precise fee and included services should be determined after discussions with universities and validation of their needs and budgets.

### 10.5. Pre-admission service fees

Formal pre-admission requests may carry a fair fee for each participating university.

The student's **first-choice university is exempt from the SmartParcours pre-admission service fee**, according to the platform's defined first-choice policy. Additional university pre-admission requests may incur a fee for each institution.

The platform must clearly distinguish this service fee from any university application fee, tuition deposit, or other institutional charge.

An expression of interest that does not initiate the formal pre-admission process remains separate and does not require payment for that process.

The official verified academic profile is required for formal pre-admission.

### 10.6. Teachers and professionals

Teachers and professionals can register and use their core platform features for free.

This supports participation in Community, knowledge sharing, and student guidance without requiring individual contributors to purchase access.

Any future paid services for these users would need a separate rationale and should not be assumed as part of the initial business model.

### 10.7. Scholarship partnerships and sponsorship

SmartParcours can work with scholarship providers, NGOs, educational organizations, and companies to support access to opportunities.

These partnerships may create additional revenue or funding for educational initiatives while helping students discover scholarships and other forms of support.

Sponsors should have clearly defined access rights and selection criteria. Sponsored opportunities must be distinguished from general orientation recommendations.

### 10.8. Business model principles

The business model should remain transparent and affordable.

Students should not need to pay to create a profile, access the core community, explore university information, or obtain their basic orientation allowance.

Paid features should provide additional value without turning educational guidance into a system where a student's financial resources determine their perceived ability or future potential.

## 11. School Accounts: Verification and School Identity Management

SmartParcours retains school accounts, but their responsibilities are limited to institutional identity, relevant student verification, and school-level information.

Schools do not need to manage the entire student experience.

### 11.1. School registration and verification

A school cannot create a fully recognized institutional profile merely because an individual claims to represent it.

SmartParcours must confirm the institution and the authority of the person requesting the account before activating institutional capabilities.

The registration and verification process should collect and review information such as:

- Official school name.
- School address and location.
- Official contact information.
- Director's name.
- Director's phone number.
- Director's email address.
- Relevant evidence that the applicant is authorized to represent the school.
- Other necessary institutional identifying information.

The exact evidence required may vary according to the school and the available documentation.

### 11.2. School profile

The School Profile page allows authorized representatives to:

- View the school's recognized information.
- Add or update the school logo.
- Maintain permitted institutional details.
- View aggregate statistics about associated students.
- Access the list of students associated with the school, according to the platform's permissions.
- Review pending verification requests.
- Track new, completed, or otherwise outstanding requests.

School-level statistics should be aggregated appropriately. The school account should not automatically expose every student's private questionnaire answers, orientation reports, or public-profile preferences.

### 11.3. Student list

Schools can view a list of students who have associated themselves with the school's recognized record.

This supports academic verification and the processing of relevant requests. It is not intended to reproduce a full school-management system.

School administrators do not need to create every student account, manage all student passwords, or enter every bulletin for the platform to function.

### 11.4. Verification requests

When a student requests a verified academic profile, the relevant school receives a request to review the academic information within its authority.

The administrator can review the information, confirm its accuracy, request corrections, or decline verification with an appropriate explanation.

After approval, SmartParcours issues a dated, versioned verified record or profile link.

The school confirms only information it can reasonably verify. It does not certify the student's interests, AI recommendations, discovery points, or every other part of the account.

### 11.5. Consistent school identities

SmartParcours maintains one canonical record for each recognized school.

Students select from the recognized list rather than independently creating school entries. Requests to add missing schools can be reviewed before new records are created.

When an authorized school representative updates an official name, the change should be reflected consistently in associated student profiles and relevant platform areas.

### 11.6. AI-assisted duplicate detection

AI can compare school names, spelling variations, addresses, locations, contact details, and other relevant identifying information to flag possible duplicate school records.

The AI only identifies possible matches. It does not automatically merge records.

A SmartParcours administrator reviews the case and may contact the schools to confirm whether the records represent the same institution.

### 11.7. School record merging

After confirming that two records refer to the same school, an authorized SmartParcours administrator can merge them into one canonical school identity.

The process must preserve relevant student associations, verification history, and audit information. It must also avoid transferring institutional permissions to the wrong representative.

### 11.8. School independence principle

Students whose schools have no SmartParcours account can still use the core platform.

They can create profiles, enter grades, complete questionnaires, receive orientation recommendations, participate in eligible communities, and explore universities.

The principal limitation is formal school verification, which may require the institution's participation or another accepted verification process.

## 12. Teacher and Professional Accounts

Teachers and professionals use a common account framework with role-specific information, verification, and permissions.

Their participation is free.

### 12.1. Professional registration

The registration process should collect the information necessary to establish the user's identity and role, including:

- Name.
- Email address.
- Phone number.
- Profession or area of expertise.
- Relevant professional information.
- Credentials or affiliations where appropriate.

The platform should verify identities and credentials to the degree appropriate for the role being claimed.

### 12.2. Teacher registration

Teachers use the same general account framework but must select their school from the recognized school list.

**School selection is mandatory for teacher registration.**

If the school is not listed, the teacher can request that it be added or reviewed rather than creating an unrestricted duplicate entry.

This allows SmartParcours to maintain a consistent school directory and establish the teacher's stated institutional affiliation.

The affiliation should not automatically imply that the school has endorsed every contribution made by the teacher.

### 12.3. Community participation

Teachers and professionals can:

- Answer student questions in permitted public spaces.
- Publish educational resources.
- Share study tips and practical knowledge.
- Participate in Knowledge Hubs.
- Contribute to orientation discussions.
- Share career and professional experience.
- Provide comments on student profiles where permitted.

### 12.4. Accompanying students

Verified teachers and professionals may participate in closer student-guidance arrangements where the student agrees and the arrangement follows SmartParcours' safety and permission rules.

The platform should distinguish general advice, ongoing mentorship, formal recommendations, and other types of support.

Direct private messaging is not available. Guidance should occur through controlled, permitted workflows and public spaces.

## 13. University Accounts and Institutional Participation

Universities have dedicated institutional accounts, subject to SmartParcours verification.

### 13.1. University registration

The university registration process should collect appropriate institutional information, such as:

- Official university name.
- Address and location.
- Official institutional email.
- Contact number.
- Authorized representative's identity and role.
- Official website and relevant institutional information.
- Other information necessary for verification and billing.

A university account becomes an official participating account only after SmartParcours has verified the institution and the relevant representative.

### 13.2. University profile and forum

Authorized representatives can manage the university profile, publish official announcements, maintain relevant programme information, and operate university forums.

Students can follow these forums to discover updates, programmes, events, and opportunities.

### 13.3. Student interest and pre-admission

Universities can view permitted lists of students who have expressed interest, according to student consent and applicable access rules.

Students can initiate formal pre-admission requests through the platform when a university supports that process.

The university makes its own admissions decisions. SmartParcours facilitates communication and status tracking but does not replace institutional authority.

### 13.4. Annual university fee

Participating universities pay a small annual fee for their presence on the platform and the agreed institutional features.

The fee structure should be simple and transparent, with additional paid services defined separately if needed.

## 14. Leaderboards and SmartParcours Performance

SmartParcours includes leaderboards to encourage participation, progress, and positive engagement.

Potential leaderboard categories include:

- Global SmartParcours performance.
- School-level participation.
- Discovery-game achievements.
- Other eligible game-specific achievements introduced in the future.

The exact scoring system should be transparent and designed to reward meaningful participation rather than wealth or academic privilege.

Leaderboards should not be based on paid credits, and paying for additional AI generations must not automatically improve a student's ranking.

School leaderboards should not disadvantage students simply because their school has fewer registered users. The platform can consider normalized measures, such as participation rates or achievement-based scores, rather than ranking schools only by raw totals.

Formal academic profiles do not need to include leaderboard results or game points. Students should be able to control whether eligible achievements appear on their public profile.

Leaderboards must also be designed with privacy and safety in mind, especially for minors. Public rankings should avoid exposing unnecessary personal or academic information.

## 15. Gender, Inclusion, and Educational Access

SmartParcours aims to improve access to educational guidance and opportunities for students from different backgrounds.

The platform may collect gender information for clearly explained statistical purposes, with an appropriate option not to disclose it.

Aggregate statistics can help evaluate:

- Who is joining and using the platform.
- Participation in discovery activities.
- Differences in engagement across topics.
- Possible barriers to participation.
- Where outreach or additional support may be useful.

Gender should not determine available interests, games, orientation recommendations, or opportunities.

The platform should avoid exposing sensitive individual information or publishing statistics that could identify a student.

## 16. Mobile-First and Responsive Design

SmartParcours is mobile-first because students may primarily access the platform through smartphones.

The mobile interface should provide the complete core experience, not a reduced version of the desktop product.

### Mobile priorities

- Clear, compact navigation.
- Readable text and accessible controls.
- Questionnaires that can be completed in stages.
- Saved progress.
- Simple bulletin uploads and grade correction.
- Profile-sharing links that work well on mobile.
- Community discussions that are easy to read.
- Clear subscription and credit information.
- Efficient loading and careful use of mobile data.
- Simple verification and support workflows.

### Desktop experience

The desktop interface should make good use of larger screens for academic tables, detailed orientation reports, university comparisons, school verification queues, institutional dashboards, and moderation tools.

The same underlying features and permissions should remain consistent across devices.

### Accessibility

The platform should use clear language, readable typography, adequate contrast, accessible controls, and understandable feedback.

French and English should be supported consistently throughout the main interface, with clear indications where particular documents or external information are available in only one language.

## 17. Security, Privacy, and Trust

SmartParcours handles academic and personal information, including information belonging to minors. Trust is a fundamental product requirement.

Key principles include:

- Private-by-default student information.
- Clear profile-sharing controls.
- Restricted access to detailed academic records.
- Verified school and university identities where required.
- Appropriate professional identity checks.
- Secure handling of uploaded documents.
- Clear retention and deletion policies.
- Auditable academic verification.
- No direct messaging between users.
- Moderation and reporting tools.
- Age-appropriate safeguards for adult-student interactions.
- Clear separation between verified facts, human opinions, and AI-generated guidance.

Students should understand what information is used for orientation, what is shared with institutions, and what becomes visible when they publish a profile or submit a request.

The platform should collect only information necessary for its services and avoid retaining sensitive information without a defined purpose.

## 18. Business Model and Sustainability

The initial business model combines free student access, paid additional AI generations, university participation fees, and formal pre-admission service fees.

### Students

Students use SmartParcours for free, with three AI recommendation generations per trimester. Additional generations can be purchased through optional credits.

The platform should preserve access to core orientation, academic profile features, university browsing, and community participation without a subscription requirement.

### Universities

Universities pay a small annual fee to participate in SmartParcours and maintain their institutional presence.

### Pre-admission

A fair service fee applies to formal pre-admission requests at participating universities, with the student's first-choice university exempt from the SmartParcours service fee under the defined policy.

A formal pre-admission request requires a verified academic profile. A simple expression of interest does not initiate the formal pre-admission process.

### Teachers and professionals

Teacher and professional accounts are free, supporting knowledge sharing and student guidance.

### Scholarship sponsors

SmartParcours seeks partnerships with scholarship providers and other educational sponsors to help students discover opportunities and to identify outstanding candidates using transparent, agreed criteria.

### Future revenue opportunities

Additional revenue sources may be considered later, provided they fit the platform's mission and do not undermine fair access or trust.

The exact pricing, first-choice policy, university packages, payment procedures, and credit rules should be validated with students, families, and institutional partners before commercial launch.

## 19. AI's Role Across SmartParcours

AI is a central capability of SmartParcours, with personalized orientation as its most important function.

| Area | Role of AI |
|---|---|
| My Orientation | Analyze academic and personal information and generate personalized recommendations. |
| Human-supported orientation | Incorporate appropriate, authorized teacher and professional feedback as contextual input. |
| Academic records | Extract and organize bulletin information for student review. |
| Explore Universities | Help students find and compare programmes using reliable information. |
| Documentation | Help users find explanations about using SmartParcours. |
| School records | Flag possible duplicate schools for human review. |
| Community | Potentially assist with resource discovery and moderation workflows. |
| Support | Help classify questions and identify relevant support documentation. |

AI must not independently certify academic records, verify school identities, decide admissions, or make consequential judgments about students.

The system should explain the limitations of its recommendations and distinguish AI analysis from human opinions and verified academic facts.

## 20. Future Development: Discover and Additional Gamified Learning

The Discover experience remains an important part of the long-term SmartParcours vision, but it is **planned for a later development phase**.

The priority is to establish the core platform and its essential workflows first: student profiles, orientation, academic verification, Community, university accounts, documentation, subscriptions, support, and the necessary administrative tools.

Once these foundations are established, SmartParcours can introduce the discovery game and expand it over time.

### 20.1. Initial discovery concept

The game will help students explore how familiar interests connect to technology, science, and professional fields.

For example, a student interested in hair styling might explore hair science, design, digital imaging, AI-assisted applications, software, and product development through interactive cards and challenges.

Students can assemble relevant elements, receive explanatory feedback, save their progress, and earn Discovery Points.

### 20.2. Three-year structured journey

The initial plan focuses on 6e, 5e, and 4e, with two planned topics per school year and six topics across the three years.

The first two trimesters provide the planned activities. The final trimester is reserved primarily for free exploration or continuing unfinished activities, allowing students to focus on school examinations and other priorities.

Students can save progress and resume at any time. Students who join in 5e or 4e can start immediately without being penalized for missing earlier topics.

### 20.3. Points and future games

Students can earn points through participation, exploration, improvement, and completion milestones.

Future games may include programming, AI, logical reasoning, digital creativity, and other skill-building experiences.

The core discovery journey should remain accessible without payment. Points should reward participation rather than represent intelligence, academic merit, or future potential.

### 20.4. Leaderboard integration

The discovery game can contribute eligible achievements to global or school-level SmartParcours leaderboards.

Paid credits must not automatically increase leaderboard rankings, and formal academic profiles do not need to include game performance.

## 22. Measuring Success

SmartParcours should measure meaningful educational value, not only account creation or AI usage.

Relevant indicators include:

- Students who complete and revisit orientation reports.
- Usefulness and quality of AI recommendations.
- The value added by teacher and professional feedback.
- Successful academic verification requests.
- Reliability of bulletin processing.
- Student access regardless of school participation.
- Quality and usefulness of community contributions.
- Engagement with educational resources.
- University participation and student interest.
- Pre-admission process completion.
- Scholarship opportunities discovered or accessed.
- Safety, trust, and user satisfaction.
- Affordability and business sustainability.
- Participation across different student backgrounds.

Once Discover is launched, its evaluation can also consider activity completion, return rates, topic diversity, and whether students discover educational fields they had not previously considered.

Any claim that the platform improves educational access or broadens participation in STEM should be evaluated using appropriate evidence rather than inferred from usage statistics alone.

## 23. Conclusion

SmartParcours is an intelligent student orientation, academic identity, and educational opportunity platform.

It brings together student profiles, academic records, AI-assisted orientation, human guidance, universities, community knowledge sharing, and formal academic verification in one accessible environment.

Students remain independent of school participation. Schools help verify academic information. Teachers and professionals contribute knowledge and feedback. Universities publish opportunities and participate in admission-related workflows. Scholarship sponsors help connect promising students with funding opportunities.

The platform is free for students to use, with a defined allowance for AI recommendation generations and optional paid additional generations. Universities contribute through annual participation fees and eligible pre-admission services. Teachers and professionals participate without an account fee.

AI is the core intelligence of SmartParcours, but it works best when combined with reliable information, transparent reasoning, and relevant human feedback.

The future Discover experience will extend the platform by helping students connect their interests to technology and other educational fields. It will be introduced after the essential platform foundations are established.

SmartParcours does not exist to decide what students should become. It exists to help them understand themselves, explore possibilities, find trustworthy guidance, and connect with opportunities that can help them progress.

**SmartParcours — Understand yourself. Discover your possibilities. Build your future.**
